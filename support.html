How to play

Crazy Rummy is played over 13 rounds (or 7 in a half game), and the wild card changes every round: aces are wild in round 1, twos in round 2, and so on up to kings in round 13.

  • Everyone starts with 7 cards. On your turn, pick up from the deck or the discard pile, then end by discarding one card.
  • Lay down sets (3 or 4 of the same number) or runs (3 or more in a row, same suit). Wild cards can stand in for any card.
  • Once you've laid down, you can add cards to anyone's sets and runs, or swap a real card for a wild one on the table.
  • The first player to empty their hand finishes the round. Everyone else scores the cards left in their hand. The lowest total after the last round wins.

New to the game? Tap Practice game on the welcome screen, or find How to play in Settings.

Common questions

Do I need the internet to play?

No. The game plays fully offline. When you're online, it may quietly check for a newer version of the game.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Your tokens, trophies, card decks, statistics and any game in progress are saved in the app automatically. There's no account, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I unlock new places and card decks?

Win games to earn tokens, then spend them in the shop (the coin at the top of the screen) on new places and upgrades. Card decks unlock by completing challenges; you'll find them all under Card deck in Settings.

How do I turn the sounds off?

Tap the speaker button at the top right. In places with background sounds, you can switch each sound on or off separately.

The cards are hard to tell apart or too small.

In Settings you can choose four-colour or colour-blind friendly cards, and a larger card size for the cards on the table.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Choose Friends under Play against when starting a new game, and pass the phone around (2 to 6 players). Friends games are just for fun and don't count towards your stats or trophies.
