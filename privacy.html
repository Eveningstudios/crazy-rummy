Privacy Policy

Crazy Rummy – Cozy Cards · Last updated 22 October 2026

In short: Crazy Rummy does not collect, store or share any personal information. There's no account or sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is kept on your own device.

Who we are

Crazy Rummy – Cozy Cards ("the game") is made by Cozy Cards. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Cozy Cards.

Information we collect

None. The game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it doesn't use the camera or microphone.

If you type a player name into the game (for example for "pass the phone" games), it stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Your progress stays on your device

The game saves your settings, any game in progress, your tokens, trophies, card decks and statistics in the app's own storage on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

No adverts, analytics or tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking of any kind. We don't build profiles, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Internet connection and game updates

The game plays fully offline. When your device is online, the app may briefly check whether a newer version of the game is available, so that fixes can reach you quickly. This check uses Expo, a service provided by 650 Industries, Inc. It sends only basic technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as your device type (iPhone or Android) and the app's version. As with any internet connection, the server can also see your device's IP address while it answers the request. This information isn't used by us to identify you. You can read Expo's privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy.

Crash reports from Apple or Google

If you've chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us anonymous crash reports and general usage figures. These don't identify you, and we only use them to find and fix problems in the game.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if the game ever adds features that need information), we'll update this page and the date at the top before those changes take effect.
